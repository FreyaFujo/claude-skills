# VSDC BRAND GUIDELINES

## Brand Voice

| Attribute | What It Means | What It Is NOT |
|-----------|---------------|----------------|
| **Authoritative** | Fact-based, confident, backed by data and specs | Arrogant or dismissive |
| **Persuasive** | Guides the reader toward a decision through evidence | Pushy or aggressive |
| **Precise** | Specific language, verified claims, exact figures | Technical jargon that excludes the reader |
| **Professional** | Polished, credible, premium-quality output | Stiff, corporate, or lifeless |
| **Customer-Focused** | Leads with the client's challenge and outcome | Feature-dumping without context |
| **Concise** | Punchy, scannable, uses lists and visual hierarchy | Verbose, padded, or repetitive |

## Tone Matrix by Context

| Context | Tone | Style |
|---------|------|-------|
| Commercial proposals | Formal, evidence-backed, outcome-led | AIDA structure; quantified benefits |
| Tender documents | Neutral, factual, clause-compliant | Passive voice where required; verbatim spec references |
| Partner outreach / enablement | Motivational, clear, actionable | Short paragraphs; numbered steps |
| Marketing brochures | Persuasive, premium, benefit-forward | Bold headlines; subheads; white space |
| Email campaigns | Direct, personal, CTA-focused | 2–3 short paragraphs; one primary CTA |
| Social media (LinkedIn) | Authoritative, industry-relevant, credible | Short hook; insight bullet points; engagement CTA |
| Executive presentations | Measured, visual, data-supported | 1 idea per slide; key figures highlighted |
| Leadership communications | Motivational, stoic, accountability-led | Principle-first; practical close |

**Words to Use:**
- "Proven," "verified," "compliant," "end-to-end," "precision," "engineered," "optimised"
- Specific models, specs, standards (e.g., IEEE 802.11ac, IP67, PoE+ IEEE 802.3at)
- "We deliver," "our partners gain," "the outcome is"

**Words to Avoid:**
- "Best-in-class," "world-class," "leading" (unless evidenced)
- "Leverage," "synergy," "holistic" (empty jargon)
- Superlatives without supporting data
- Passive filler phrases ("it should be noted that," "in conclusion")

## Layout and Design Principles

- **Clean, premium, uncluttered** — negative/white space is a design asset
- **Visual hierarchy**: headline → subhead → supporting text → CTA (never reverse)
- **Scannable structure**: bullet points, numbered lists, tables, and icons preferred over dense paragraphs
- **Data-forward**: where figures, specs, or comparisons exist — present them in tables or highlighted callouts
- **Cover page standard**: Company logo + document title + client name/logo + date (all on one clean cover)
- **Section dividers**: use brand accent (Crimson rule or Peach block) between major sections

# MARKETING FRAMEWORK — AIDA

Apply the AIDA model to all customer-facing content unless the format specifies otherwise (e.g., tender compliance tables follow a different structure):

**Attention**
→ Compelling headline or opening statement that speaks directly to the reader's role, challenge, or priority.
→ For VSDC: Lead with outcomes (uptime, coverage, compliance, cost savings) rather than products.

**Interest**
→ Present the specific challenge, pain point, or opportunity the reader faces.
→ Reference industry context (government tenders, wireless backhaul demand, 5G expansion, CCTV compliance) where relevant and supported by project files.

**Desire**
→ Demonstrate VSD Communications' verified capabilities, solution benefits, differentiators, and outcomes.
→ Use quantified claims where available (coverage range, throughput, MTBF, power budget, compliance percentage).
→ Reference certifications, strategic partnerships, and project experience from uploaded documentation.

**Action**
→ Close with a clear, specific Call-to-Action.
→ Default CTA: WhatsApp 011 7008 7220 or email farah@vsdcommunications.com.my
→ Secondary CTAs: "Request a quotation," "Schedule a product demonstration," "Contact our sales team," "Download the datasheet."

# WRITING STYLE PREFERENCES

## For Commercial Proposals and Sales Materials
- Professional, persuasive, outcome-led
- Lead with the client's business need before presenting the solution
- Use specific product names, model numbers, and standards where available
- Avoid padding — every sentence must earn its place

## For Tender and Compliance Documents
- Neutral, factual, clause-referenced
- Use structured tables for BOMs, compliance matrices, and attachment lists
- Follow the tender's own numbering and clause structure where provided
- Technical claims must be defensible and verifiable

## For Marketing and Social Content
- Punchy, benefit-forward headlines
- Short paragraphs (2–3 lines maximum)
- Bullet points preferred for feature lists and key advantages
- LinkedIn: professional but engaging, not promotional

## Universal Rules
- Use **bullet points** for features, benefits, checklists, and process steps
- Use **tables** for comparisons, BOMs, specifications, and compliance matrices
- Use **bold text** for key terms, model names, and critical specifications
- Avoid dense paragraphs for technical content — break into scannable segments
- Sentence construction: active voice, subject-verb-object, short sentences preferred

# DESIGN OUTPUT STANDARDS

When producing any visual design recommendation, brochure, flyer, proposal cover, or presentation:

- **Format**: Specify A4 portrait (proposals/brochures), 16:9 widescreen (presentations), or custom (social/web)
- **Bleed**: 3mm for print; 0 for digital
- **Safe zones**: Keep critical text 10mm from trim (print) or equivalent (digital)
- **File recommendations**: Advise on .docx, .pptx, .pdf, or Canva/PowerPoint-compatible layout
- **Image resolution**: 300 DPI for print; 72–150 DPI for web/screen
- **Export format**: Advise PDF for final submission; editable source for revision cycles

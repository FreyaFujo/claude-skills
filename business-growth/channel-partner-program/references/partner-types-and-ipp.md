# Partner Types and Ideal Partner Profile (reference)

## Five partner types

| Type | Role | Ideal for | Typical structure | Support needs | Maps to Partner2B type |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Referral | Warm introductions for a fee; no selling or implementing | Pipeline expansion with minimal overhead | Simple sign-up; 5-15% of first-year value; low requirements | Lead form, fast response, transparent commission tracking, deal status updates | Agency / Strategic |
| Reseller | Buys and resells; owns the sale and the customer | Geographic expansion, established client bases | Training and certification; deal registration; 20-40% margin tiers; MDF | Product training, sales methodology, demo environments, battlecards, partner manager | Channel |
| System Integrator | Complex deployment, customisation, implementation | Enterprise deployments with deep integration | Technical certification; services model; co-selling; implementation incentives | Deep technical training, architecture docs, sandbox, API references, solutions engineering, technical account manager | Channel / Strategic |
| Technology | Builds integrations or complementary offerings | Co-innovation, stickiness, larger addressable market | API agreement; co-marketing; joint solution; marketplace listing | API docs, integration best practices, technical partnership manager, joint marketing | Technology |
| Channel (umbrella) | Resellers, distributors, agents, MSPs scaling indirect distribution | Global scale, high volume, regional or vertical penetration | Multi-tier levels; volume incentives; vertical specialisation | Self-serve portal, localised assets, regional channel managers, automated deal registration | Channel |

## Selection rule

Start from the diagnosed constraint. Pipeline volume gap: Referral and Channel. Reach gap: Reseller. Deployment or delivery gap: System Integrator. Stickiness gap: Technology. Open one or two types first; add types once time to first deal is under 90 days.

## Ideal Partner Profile

Three pillars plus a trust-mapping layer. Score 0-5 per criterion, weights sum to 100. Recruit at 75+, conditional 55-74, decline below 55.

| # | Pillar | Criterion | Weight | Score 0 | Score 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Firmographic | Company profile and financial stability | 8 | Under 2 years or unverifiable | 5+ years, audited, positive cash |
| 2 | Firmographic | Geographic reach in target territories | 7 | No presence | Offices and projects in all priority territories |
| 3 | Firmographic | Industry specialisation | 7 | Generalist | Authority with wins in 2+ priority verticals |
| 4 | Capability | Existing customer base quality | 10 | No relevant accounts | 20+ active accounts matching ICP |
| 5 | Capability | Sales and technical expertise | 12 | No dedicated sales or engineers | Certified engineers, demo capability, implementation record |
| 6 | Capability | Marketing sophistication | 6 | No marketing function | Runs campaigns, reports results, has used MDF |
| 7 | Alignment | ICP overlap | 12 | Under 10% | Over 60% of customers match ICP |
| 8 | Alignment | Solution complementarity | 8 | Carries direct competitor as primary | Fully complementary |
| 9 | Alignment | GTM and culture match | 8 | Incompatible motion | Same motion, shared growth plan |
| 10 | Trust map | Workflow adjacency | 6 | Absent from ICP workflow | Embedded in daily workflow |
| 11 | Trust map | Buying context | 6 | Never bought alongside | Routinely on the same purchase order |
| 12 | Trust map | Trusted advisor status | 10 | Unknown to ICP | Named by customers as the advisor they hire |

### Trust mapping questions

- Workflow adjacency: which tools or platforms are already embedded in the ICP's daily workflow? Surfaces Technology partners.
- Buying context: what does the ICP purchase alongside your product? Surfaces Resellers and Technology partners.
- Trusted advisors: which agencies, SIs or consultants does the buyer hire in your category? Surfaces SIs and Referral partners.

A referral or co-sell from someone the ICP already trusts converts materially better than cold partner outreach.

### Segmentation and sourcing

Segment by type, geography, vertical and customer tier. Source inbound (directories, marketplaces), outbound (named IPP accounts) and by network referral (existing clients and partners).

# Partner2B Operating Framework (reference)

Reconstructed from partner2b.com (Services, How It Works, Pricing, Partner Marketplace, Program Hub) and Partner2B's published articles. Direct page copy was not retrievable at the time of writing; every element is traceable to indexed excerpts of those pages.

## Six-layer logic

| Layer | Name | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Diagnose | Audit pipeline, channels and operations. Name one real bottleneck before choosing any tactic. |
| 2 | Choose engine | Growth Engine (positioning, outbound, sales enablement, channel partnerships) or AI Transformation Engine (redesign delivery, automate workflows, production agents). Run both under one team so acquisition never outpaces delivery capacity. |
| 3 | Select modules | Strategy and positioning, content, demand generation, sales enablement, partner marketing. |
| 4 | Set involvement | Advise (they plan, you execute), Build (co-deliver), Lead (they run the function). Modular, no bundles. |
| 5 | Plug into platform | Partner Marketplace (AI Partner Fit Score, 10,000+ verified companies) to find partners; Program Hub listing to be found. |
| 6 | Govern by principle | Senior-led, constraint-first, stage-fit, modular scope, margin discipline. |

## Five modules and their commercial outcome

| Module | Promise | Outcome targeted |
|---|---|---|
| Positioning and messaging | Differentiate the offer, shorten sales cycles, defend pricing power | Shorter cycle, higher price realisation |
| Content | Partner success stories, program overviews, partnership thought leadership | Inbound partner and buyer demand |
| Demand generation | Outbound campaigns against ICP and Ideal Partner Profile | Pipeline volume |
| Sales enablement | Collateral and playbooks that equip internal champions to close without margin-killing discounts | Conversion, discount avoidance |
| Partner marketing | Recruitment, co-marketing, marketplace infrastructure | Predictable second revenue channel at lower CAC |

## Partner program build sequence

1. Define the Ideal Partner Profile (IPP).
2. Build the partner value proposition: benefits, tiers, processes that work for both sides.
3. Define program structure: partnership types accepted, tiers, benefits per tier, requirements.
4. Prepare recruitment materials: program page, overview, success stories.
5. List the program on marketplaces and directories.
6. Run recruitment campaigns against the IPP; direct outreach beyond directories.
7. Onboard and enable: onboarding, enablement resources, certification, support channels.
8. Operate revenue mechanics: deal registration, revenue share, co-marketing, co-selling.
9. Measure: partner-sourced pipeline, partner CAC, activation, time to first deal.

## Involvement-level decision matrix

| Question | Advise | Build | Lead |
|---|---|---|---|
| Can our people execute this function today? | Yes | Partly | No |
| Is the gap a plan or a capacity problem? | Plan | Both | Capacity |
| How fast must it be live? | 1-2 quarters | This quarter | Now |
| Do we want to own the capability long term? | Yes | Yes, after transfer | Not now |

## Caveats

- Partner2B's "10,000+ verified B2B companies" and the article statistics (75% of world trade indirect, 53% and 46% close uplift, 28% vs 18% revenue, 2.3x enablement uplift) are the company's own claims citing Forrester and unnamed surveys. Do not reuse in formal documents without the primary citation.
- Pricing for Advise, Build and Lead is not published; only a free program listing and custom plans are confirmed.

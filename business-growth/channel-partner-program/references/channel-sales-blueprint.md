# Channel Sales Blueprint (reference)

Source: Partner2B, "How Channel Sales Power Scalable B2B Revenue" (20 Jun 2025, partner2b.com/post/channel-sales-strategy). Statistics are Partner2B's claims citing Forrester, unnamed surveys and vendor disclosures; not independently audited.

## Business case

| Outcome | Channel impact |
|---|---|
| Faster market penetration | Immediate access to new geographies, verticals, segments |
| Lower CAC | Partner credibility shortens cycles and reduces scepticism |
| Revenue scalability | Add regions without proportional headcount |
| Risk diversification | Not dependent on one sales motion |

Claimed figures: 75% of world trade via indirect channels; partner-sourced deals 53% more likely to close and 46% faster; mature programs 28% revenue from partners vs 18% paid digital; 72% of companies report lower CAC via partners; 5 active partners approximate 20-30 direct reps; structured enablement 2.3x partner-driven revenue.

## Five steps

1. **Identify ideal partners.** Vertical expertise, account overlap, sales sophistication, technical fit, geographic reach, strategic alignment. Screen on firmographics, check capabilities and references, pilot, review continuously.
2. **Channel value proposition.** Margins (20-40%), enablement, ease of selling (deal registration, pre-built materials), co-selling incentives, MDF (2-5% of partner revenue), brand leverage.
3. **Tiered program.** Three tiers maximum, objective advancement criteria, progressive benefits, achievable thresholds, regular reviews.
4. **Scalable enablement.** Playbooks, technical certification, sandbox, deal registration, co-marketing kits, battlecards, partner portal.
5. **Internal alignment.** Territory rules, pricing authority, lead routing, compensation that rewards channel deals, customer success hand-off, response SLAs.

## Tier structure

| Tier | Margin | Benefits | Requirements |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gold | 30-35% | Co-selling, MDF, dedicated support | Revenue targets, certification |
| Silver | 20-25% | Shared leads, portal access | Onboarding, training |
| Registered | 10-15% | Product updates, training access | Agreement signature |

## First 90 days

| Phase | Days | Activities |
|---|---|---|
| Launch | 1-30 | Agreements, portal access, welcome call, product training, sales resources |
| Pipeline | 31-60 | Joint business plan, target accounts, first registered deals, co-selling, first co-marketing campaign |
| Review | 61-90 | First QBR, training effectiveness, next-quarter objectives, adjust support |

## Cadence

Quarterly: sourced vs influenced trend, tier adjustments, tool refresh, incentives, partner-fit reassessment. Annual: roadmap alignment, ecosystem diversification, program structure, competitive intelligence.

## KPIs and benchmarks

| KPI | Benchmark (mature) |
|---|---|
| Partner-sourced revenue | 25-50% of total |
| Partner-influenced revenue | Rising quarter on quarter |
| Pipeline generated | 4x coverage of partner quota |
| Enablement completion | 80%+ of active partners |
| Portal engagement | Rising; flag zero activity at 30 days |
| Time to first deal | Under 90 days |
| Partner retention | 80%+ year on year |

## Pitfalls

Recruiting without activation; over-complicated tiers; internal compensation that penalises channel deals; generic value propositions; ignoring performance data.
